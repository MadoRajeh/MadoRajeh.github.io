About me

The person behind the work

I am Ahmad, a Filipino based in the Philippines, and the person businesses hand their whole marketing back office to. I started in data analysis 5+ years ago, and the work kept growing: today I run Meta ad campaigns, write SEO pages that target local searches, design social graphics, edit video, and build the automated reporting that keeps it all honest.

Right now that means managing marketing end to end for a fifteen-location preschool network: their ads, their monthly content, their SEO pages, and the systems that reconcile their leads. One point of contact instead of four freelancers, and a lot fewer tabs open for you.

I work fast, communicate straight, and measure success in hours you get back.
