CRM training guides

The least technical thing in this portfolio, and the one with the highest return per hour spent. No dashboard survives bad input.

The problem

The reports were wrong before they were built

Statuses were left blank. The source was typed into the status field. Leads were marked enrolled before paperwork was signed. Every one of those is a data-entry habit, not a software fault, and no amount of engineering downstream can recover a fact that was never recorded.

The engineering

The decisions that actually mattered

Diagnose from the data, not from a theory

The three mistakes in the guide are the three that actually appeared in the records, found by looking at the rows. Guessing at what people get wrong produces a guide that addresses problems nobody has.

Say what breaks, not just what to do

"Don't leave the status blank" is an instruction. "A blank lead is invisible to every report — it isn't counted as lost, it isn't counted at all" is a reason. People follow reasons and forget instructions.

Show the screen, don't describe it

The guide contains the form with the one field the reports read highlighted. A paragraph describing where to click is a paragraph that gets skipped.

Four minutes, or it doesn't get read

Written to be read once, standing up, by someone with a phone ringing. Length was a design constraint, not an afterthought.
