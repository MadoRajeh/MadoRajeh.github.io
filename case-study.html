01
The situation

Four freelancers. No shared source of truth.

A 15-location preschool network was running marketing through four different freelancers and no shared source of truth. Nobody could say, with confidence, which location was actually working, or where the budget was quietly being wasted.

02
What was actually going wrong

The reports were lying, just a little.

Leads were coming in that the ad platform's own tracking never counted, which meant cost-per-lead looked worse than it actually was, and budget decisions were being made on bad numbers. Meanwhile, the weekly report ate half a workday, every week, by hand.

03 What I built

One coordinated system, not four disconnected habits.

A CRM sync that self-repairs instead of needing a person to babysit it. A weekly scorecard that judges every location by one honest number. A dashboard that answers "how are we doing?" on the spot.

04
Why it works this way

Judged by one number. Fixes itself.

The scorecard judges every location by one number, cost per lead, not ten vanity metrics nobody acts on. The sync fixes itself when something breaks, because a system that needs a person to babysit it isn't really automation.
